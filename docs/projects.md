# Projects

## Geospatial

- **[MapLab](https://noahweidig.com/maplab)**: A simple GIS software for non-GIS users.
- **[GIS Convert](https://gisconvert.vercel.app/)**: Browser-based GIS file conversion with no upload.
- **[Map Explorer](https://noahweidig.com/maps)**: A collection of interactive maps.
- **[TIGER Roads](https://gee-community-catalog.org/projects/tiger_roads/)**: US Census TIGER roads in Google Earth Engine.

## Wildfire

- **[WUI Wildfire Explorer](https://ee-weidignc.projects.earthengine.app/view/wui-wildfire)**: A GEE app for visualizing risk.
- **[FPA-FOD Wildfire Ignitions](https://noahweidig.com/fpa-fod/)**: Interactive map of 33 years of U.S. wildfire ignition points.
- **[Wildfire Risk](https://rpubs.com/noahweidig/us-wildfire-risk)**: Wildfire risk visualizer by state.
- **[Eastern Fire Network](https://www.easternfirenetwork.org/)**: Website for a wildfire research network.
- **[FireR](https://noahweidig.com/fireR/)**: An R package for U.S. fire data.
