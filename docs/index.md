## About

I'm an ecologist and GIS analyst specializing in geospatial data science, environmental modeling, and large-scale spatial analysis. I'm currently a GIS & Remote Sensing Research Associate in the Donovan Lab at the University of Florida.

I hold an M.S. in Forest Resources & Conservation (University of Florida) and a B.S. in Biological Sciences (Northern Kentucky University), both with a 4.0 GPA.
